Geo Tracking

Production Flutter app for field sales teams with offline workflows, GPS validation, and ERP synchronization.

About the Project

Built a production Flutter application for sales representatives and distribution teams, enabling them to manage daily routes, customer visits, POS orders, payments, returns, stock, invoices, and reports even with unstable internet connectivity.

Tools & Technologies

  • Flutter
  • Dart
  • Offline-first
  • GPS / Maps
  • Odoo ERP
  • SQLite
  • Bluetooth Printing
  • Arabic / English UI

Key Features

  • Offline order, payment, and visit creation with automatic sync
  • GPS check-in/out with distance validation and anti-mock-location checks
  • Odoo ERP integration for products, customers, stock, payments, loyalty, and reports
  • Offline request dashboard for pending, failed, and archived sync operations
  • Route visualization with maps, customer pins, and live location updates
  • Sales analytics, top products, sales vs returns, and visit-status dashboards
  • Barcode scanning, receipt/signature handling, and Bluetooth thermal printing
  • Arabic/English localized experience
